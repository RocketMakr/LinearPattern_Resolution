Today I made time to research and comb through the Intro To Electronics guide to make sure I have a good understanding of RC timing. And I found lots of interest an primary example for RC timing shown here:

![image.png](image.png)

And I decided to use this concept to create a pattern of LEDs that turn on at the same time but turn off sequentially.

As for how I wanted to sequence to act, I thought about films where street lights turn off one by one as the protagonist goes down a street to give a feeling of suspense or mystery. I wanted to replicate this process as an LED pattern where each LED turns off as you move down the circuit.
